# Chapter One – The Lighthouse Keeper

Mara kept the lighthouse on the northern rocks, where the sea argued with the wind every night.

She had never seen a ship come close enough to need her light. She lit it anyway.

On the first evening of autumn, a small boat appeared at the edge of the fog, rowing towards the rocks as if it knew the way.

# Chapter Two – The Visitor

The rower was a girl of perhaps twelve, carrying a lantern that gave no light at all.

"I'm told you keep the last lamp," she said. "Mine went out."

Mara looked at the dark lantern for a long time. Then she opened the door and let her in.
