# Episode 2 – Let There Be Light

## Darkness First

For three weeks, the triangle lived in perfect darkness. Not because it was meant to, but because nobody had told the engine what light was.

<!-- visual: flat-shaded triangle on black -->
The first attempt at lighting made everything white. The second made everything black. The third made the triangle glow like a warning sign.

## The Normal Problem

It turned out the normals were pointing inwards. Every surface was politely facing away from the sun.

<!-- visual: debug view with normals drawn as little arrows -->
One flipped sign later, the triangle had a bright side and a dark side, like every good villain.

## Closing

It was still just a triangle. But now it could see the sun.
